# 📚 Class 11 Syllabus Tracker

A simple, powerful, and offline-ready study management app designed to help Class 11 students organise their syllabus, track academic progress, plan daily studies, and stay consistent throughout the academic year.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 1. Dashboard
- View overall syllabus completion.
- Track completed chapters across all six subjects.
- Get a quick overview of your academic progress.
- View study activity and important statistics.

### 📖 2. Syllabus Management
Track your Class 11 syllabus across six subjects:

- Physics
- Chemistry
- Mathematics
- English
- Odia
- Statistics

For each chapter, you can:
- Update its learning status.
- Add personal notes and formula notes.
- Create and track subtopics.
- Set revision dates.
- Monitor individual chapter progress.

### 📅 3. Daily Study Planner
- Create daily study tasks.
- Assign subjects and chapters to tasks.
- Set target dates and estimated study time.
- Track completed and pending tasks.

### ⏱️ 4. Pomodoro Focus Timer
- Built-in 25-minute focus timer.
- Study and break sessions.
- Automatic study-session logging.
- Sound notifications.
- Track your focused study time.

### 🔄 5. Revision Management
- Keep track of chapters that need revision.
- Set upcoming revision dates.
- Identify chapters that require extra attention.

### 📈 6. Study Analytics
- View subject-wise completion.
- Monitor your study progress.
- Check your recent study activity.
- Track completed chapters and study sessions.

### 💾 7. Backup & Restore
Your academic progress matters. That's why the app includes:
- Export complete backups as JSON files.
- Copy backup data as raw JSON text.
- Restore progress from a JSON file.
- Restore using copied JSON text.
- Backup validation and restore confirmation.

## 🌐 Offline Support

The app is designed to work without an internet connection.

- Works offline after the app has been loaded successfully.
- Can be installed as an Android APK.
- Uses local storage to save your academic progress.
- Supports online updates through the hosted website when the APK is configured to load that website.

## 📱 Installation

### Android APK
1. Download the APK from the repository's Releases section, if available.
2. Install it on your Android device.
3. Open the app and start tracking your syllabus.

### Web Version
Open the hosted GitHub Pages website using your browser.

## 🔐 Data Safety

Your syllabus progress, tasks, and study sessions are stored locally on your device.

**Important:**
- Export backups regularly.
- Keep a copy of your backup somewhere safe.
- Do not clear app data or uninstall the APK without backing up your progress.
- Restoring a backup replaces your current saved progress.

The app does not automatically synchronise your progress between different devices.

## 🚀 Project Goal

The goal of this project is to make studying more organised, measurable, and consistent.

Instead of wondering how much syllabus is left, students can track their progress, plan their daily studies, and focus on completing one chapter at a time.

**Study smarter. Stay consistent. Finish your syllabus.**
